\begin{abstract}
\noindent Its companion~\cite{deadly-race} derives a bar for coercion-resistant
custody and shows that designs leaving a \emph{feasible-but-unfinished} path
manufacture an incentive to kill the holder. This paper takes up the other
mechanism the same model yields, and the reason neither can be read off the
record.

Denial credibility is a \textbf{common resource}: supplied by the population of
holders, consumed by each holder who denies. A norm of concealment and coached
denial depletes it, so that a denial carries no information and the rational
continuation of an interrogation opening with one is \emph{further coercion}
rather than release. We name this the \textbf{Denial Spiral}, show it is the
Obscurity Paradox run in reverse, and show that its cost is an \emph{externality}
--- borne by whoever must be believed, including holders of designs that deplete
nothing, and including people who hold no bitcoin at all.

We then classify what the industry ships against that mechanism --- wallet-erase
PINs, duress PINs, decoy passphrase wallets, and one vendor-documented instance
of concealment marketed as deniability --- and find the class fails under
Kerckhoffs by construction, on the vendors' own documentation.

Finally we ask why a crisis this visible resists diagnosis, and give four
compounding censoring mechanisms. Three remove the evidence: the exposure
covariate is unmeasurable because obscurity does not advertise itself, the
numerator is misclassified because a robbery whose victim is killed is recorded
as a homicide, and the residue is held by bereaved families with no reason to
speak. The fourth removes the \emph{response}, and so survives publication:
conceding is privately costly to whoever concedes while recalcitrance externalizes
the cost --- a commons problem one level up, with the field's own capacity to
self-correct as the depleted resource. Coding of $352$ registry incidents grounds
the argument and bounds what it can claim.

The results are \emph{derived}, not measured, and we say why no observation could
settle them: every witness to an enacted case has an interest in the answer, so
the instrument that would test the model is disabled by the mechanism the model
describes.
\end{abstract}
