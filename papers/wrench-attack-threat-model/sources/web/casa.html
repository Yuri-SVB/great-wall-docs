THE TRUSTED SOLUTION
Self-custody with superpowers

Protection against social engineering hacks.

Multi-key security means no single compromised password, phished login, or manipulated support call can move your bitcoin. An attacker would need access to multiple keys stored on separate devices in different locations.

Protection against wrench attacks

Geographic distribution of your keys means no one can force you to hand over your bitcoin on the spot. Even under duress, you physically can't access enough keys in one place to authorize a transaction.

Protection against human errors

Misplace a key or accidentally delete a wallet? With a multi-key vault, losing one key doesn't mean losing your bitcoin. Casa helps you replace it and restore full protection - no panic, no fund loss.

Protection against device failure and supply-chain attack.

Your bitcoin is never dependent on a single hardware device. If one signing device breaks, gets compromised, or ships with a vulnerability, your remaining keys keep your funds safe while you swap it out.

Protection against natural disasters

Keys stored across multiple geographic locations mean no single fire, flood, or earthquake can wipe out access to your bitcoin. Even if one location is destroyed, your vault stays intact.
