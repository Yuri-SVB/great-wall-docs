No Dependency on Services

Rewind uses Bitcoin's native properties to freeze your funds securely, with no 3rd-party services required. Even if Rewind were to go out of business, the app remains fully operational, avoiding reliance on 3rd-party cooperation or complex recovery tools.

No Monthly Payments

Pay a one-time 0.40% fee per vault. After that, forget about recurring fees. There are no charges for funds recovery, maintenance, or regular wallet use. If you only freeze your funds once, that’s the only payment you’ll ever make. No quotas and no ongoing dependence on us.

Backups Made Simple

Forget managing complex files with descriptors, xpubs or multisig setups. Rewind automatically encrypts and backs up your wallet's technical data to an unstoppable P2P network. If we cease to operate, your data will remain available and secured by the community.
