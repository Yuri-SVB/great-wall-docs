\section{The discretion counsel, in full}
\label{app:intro}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\noindent \S\ref{sec:chain} and \S\ref{sec:classify} treat obscurity as a
defense. The prior question --- whether the records that make a holder a target
need exist at all --- is raised by the counsel to buy only outside the
identity-verified channels and leave nothing fiscally reportable behind. That
this lowers exposure we grant, as legitimate personal hygiene. The introduction's
footnote names the three things that nonetheless keep it from working as a
security mechanism; here they are in full.

\emph{First, exposure ratchets.} A record cannot be uncreated and a
leak cannot be un-leaked, so the strategy pays only \emph{ex ante}, and only for
the holder whose data has never once escaped; for everyone else there is no
longer anything protectable to protect. Worse, the two curves run against the
holder: the cost of maintaining discretion compounds with every year held and
every counterparty met, while its marginal return falls as the stock of records
already leaked grows. \emph{Second, for the compliant holder the strategy is not
lawfully available.} Where holdings or gains are legally reportable, leaving no
fiscal record is not discretion but non-declaration --- tax evasion, a criminal
offense --- so the counsel reduces to an instruction the law-abiding holder
cannot follow. Nor is the resulting record safe by virtue of sitting with the
state: in France, the jurisdiction carrying the largest share of recorded
incidents, an employee of the tax administration was placed under judicial
investigation in 2025 for allegedly consulting the authority's internal database
and passing the results to organized crime, with investigators reporting queries
aimed specifically at cryptocurrency investors~\cite{fisc-leak-2026}. The
compliant holder is thus asked to choose between an offense and a database that
has been mined for targets. \emph{Third, and decisive here}, discretion is not a
\emph{mechanism} at all: under Assumption~\ref{ass:kerckhoffs} the adversary is
granted the holder's situation, so any defense contingent on their ignorance is
out of scope by construction.
