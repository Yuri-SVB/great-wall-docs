\section{Conclusion}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Deadly Race turns ``graceful degradation'' from a virtue into a liability
whenever the degraded state is a feasible-but-slower path to the secret: that
state is a race, and the race pays an assassination incentive. The No-Gray-Area
Criterion is the resulting bar, and the reachability corollary shows exactly two
honest ways to clear it --- time-lock-rescue vaults removing the attacker's reach
at the cost of a trusted third party, and the TGPO orbit removing the race
entirely, keeping custody individual behind a floor that leaves $K$ prohibitive to
reach from every seizable state bar a bounded residual (Theorem~\ref{thm:floor}).

\paragraph{What devicelessness buys.}
Keeping the whole setup in the head is also what lets the argument stay
\emph{a fortiori} --- granting $\Attacker$ every advantage and still finding no
gray state --- and keeps the game single-shot rather than a tangle of sub-games: the deviceless holder
has a minimal window in which $K$ is seizable at all and a \emph{separate} minimal
window of deadly-race exposure, no hostage device and so none of the bluffing maze
of ransoming a seized token, no trusted third party, and no binding to sites of
custody.

\paragraph{Open items.}
Four remain: a richer utility model behind the single-shot kill increment; a
quantitative bound for the residual window (\S\ref{sec:residual-dr}); an $\Hhard$
calibration against hardware; and, softest of all, a field study establishing that
a transmissible-resistant perceptual oracle exists (\S\ref{sec:limits}).
