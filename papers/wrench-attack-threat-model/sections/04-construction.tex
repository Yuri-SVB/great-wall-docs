\section{Construction --- the Time-Gated Perceptual Oracle}
\label{sec:gw}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The taxonomy leaves one route unexhibited. We close it with a construction
clearing Criterion~\ref{prin:nga} by \ref{r:removerace} --- the \emph{tacit} route
that Corollary~\ref{cor:fourprops} shows is forced by holding all Four Properties
at once: it admits \emph{no feasible seizure path}, so custody stays individual.
Call it the \textbf{Time-Gated Perceptual Oracle} (TGPO): \emph{time-gated} for
the memory-hard orbit, \emph{perceptual} for the tacit fractal recognition,
\emph{oracle} for its evaluate-only-by-the-holder semantics. It is \emph{one} way
to clear the bar, not the paper's contribution --- \S\ref{sec:chain} and
\S\ref{sec:classify} stand whether or not it does --- so we give only what the
floor theorem needs; Appendix~\ref{app:tgpo} carries the orbit diagram, the proof
and the residual analysis, and~\cite{gw-design} the instantiation parameters.

\subsection{What the construction is}

The secret is a set of \emph{tacit} fractal locations (Assumption~\ref{ass:tkba}):
recognizable by the holder on a rendered board, but not dictatable, because a
point cannot be transmitted without the fractal parameters that render it. Those
parameters are themselves regenerated only by walking an \emph{orbit} --- a chain
of $N$ memory-hard (Argon2-class) stages seeded by a public,
precomputation-resistant timechain salt~\cite{gw-timechain-salt} --- so the deep
boards do not exist until the derivation reaches them, and the raw state of each
link is wiped in the instant between a cheap inner hash and the long memory-hard
step, leaving seizable material live only momentarily. Each stage commits a whole
Shamir polynomial~\cite{shamir1979} rather than a point, so no individual guess is
ever checkable in isolation and $\Attacker$ has no sub-threshold oracle. The
master secrets are derived by a deliberately \emph{cheap} hash at each stage,
which keeps the terminal window instant and puts the resistance in entropy: a
brute of the final stage from a bare board costs $2^{96}$, infeasible
by~\ref{s:infeasiblec}. Appendix~\ref{app:tgpo} gives the orbit equations, the
diagram, and the instantiation parameters are in~\cite{gw-design}.

\subsection{The floor}

\begin{theorem}[The floor on the master secret]\label{thm:floor}
Under Assumptions~\ref{ass:power}--\ref{ass:tkba} and the standard build of
\eqref{eq:orbit}--\eqref{eq:master}, from every seizable state \emph{save a single
residual window} (\S\ref{sec:residual-seizure}) a target master secret $K_N$ is
\emph{prohibitive} to reach --- its cost meets the floor
(Stipulation~\ref{ass:feas}). A device seizure outside that window therefore
yields nothing feasible: a \emph{clear attacker failure} in the sense of
Criterion~\ref{prin:nga}, with no racer for $\Attacker$ to reach.
\end{theorem}

\noindent The proof (Appendix~\ref{app:tgpo}) is a case analysis over seizable
states. Preimage resistance makes the orbit forward-only, so a seized board never
yields its predecessor, and reaching the target from any earlier board requires
guessing every intervening stage, each guess memory-hard-gated: pure device
seizure leaves $K$ unreachable, and a wrench of stage~$0$ or any mid-orbit seizure
leaves $K_N$ at $2^{96}$ or more. Only a live $o_N$ escapes. Exercising the oracle
on stages \emph{below} the chain adds nothing to the floor
(Remark~\ref{rem:shallow-null}); the reference implementation routes them through
it anyway, to make the ceremony \emph{observable} --- showing a protocol-ignorant
$\Attacker$, on bits whose exposure costs nothing, that the rest of the derivation
demands a faculty $\Victim$ has but cannot hand over
(Remark~\ref{rem:adversary-education}). That moves $\Attacker$'s \emph{belief},
not the impossibility.

\subsection{The tolerated residual seizure window}
\label{sec:residual-seizure}

One sliver escapes, and it is first a \emph{seizability} gap, not yet a deadly
race: a wrench that lands with the final board $o_N$ imminently or currently live
and subdues $\Victim$ before the wipe catches the derivation essentially complete
and $\Victim$ under control. There $\Attacker$ \emph{does} reach $K$ and the stash
is lost --- we concede it plainly. What keeps it a \emph{residual} is how narrow
the coincidence is: a precise landing on the brief live-$o_N$ interval, and a
subdual before any wipe or reset.

\subsection{The tolerated residual deadly-race window}
\label{sec:residual-dr}

Funds-loss is a \emph{separate} axis from the deadly race, and the latter does not
follow from the former. Killing buys $\Attacker$ only a \emph{shaving}: after the
second-to-last point of stage $N$ is surrendered, a cheap $2^{32}$ brute reaches
$K$ in ${\approx}1$ minute, versus waiting for a cooperating $\Victim$ to
recollect it. The deciding quantity is therefore not $(1-p_A)\,W$, non-negative by
definition, but the \emph{utility increment} $\Delta p_A\,W - c$ of
assassinate-then-brute over keeping the victim cooperating. Appendix~\ref{app:tgpo}
shows it negative with overwhelming probability: the gain term is bounded by a
minute the brute can be \emph{pre-staged} around, the loss term is a strict
subtraction because a victim killed before a wrong point is corrected is
$\Attacker$'s burned retry, and $c$ is murder aggravated by frivolous motive
\emph{and} by the victim's defenselessness. This is an \emph{incentive} argument
against $\mathrm{WDY}[W,c]$, not a theorem (\S\ref{sec:limits}), and the window is
in any case a six-way coincidence --- sophistication enough to drive the brute and
the interface, a landing inside the $o_N$-live window, a subdual before reset, a
judgment that immediate escape is critical, an ability to rule out a wrong
surrendered point, and acceptance of a doubly-aggravated murder. Three design
measures shrink it further: the anonymous time-lock cloaking \emph{when} it occurs
(Remark~\ref{rem:flying-turtle}), canonical-island highlighting
(Remark~\ref{rem:canonical-island}), and splitting a stash across several $K_i$
(Remark~\ref{rem:splitting}); feigning is a bonus rather than load-bearing
(Remark~\ref{rem:feigning}).
