\section{Limitations and scope}
\label{sec:limits}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\textbf{The tacit premise is unproven --- the softest assumption.} Everything on
the tacit route rests on a \emph{perceptual oracle}: a stimulus a human can
\emph{recognize} but not \emph{articulate}, which for TKBA must simultaneously
admit a binary encoding, be feasible to memorize at $\ge 96$ bits, and be
infeasible to transmit within the coercion budget $t$
(Definition~\ref{def:tacit}). None of the three is proved here; their conjunction
is an empirical, psychometric claim awaiting a field study. Perturbed
Burning-Ship fractal locations are our instantiation, but until such a study
\textbf{the validity of TKBA remains an assumption, not a result}, and it is the
softest premise in the paper. Its imperfection is precisely the residual window
(\S\ref{sec:residual-seizure}).

\textbf{Living with it is affordable, and the objection has an empirical
answer.} Whether carrying $\ge 96$ bits of tacit recall is too costly is a
separate question from whether the oracle exists; retention is
\emph{assisted}~\cite{gw-cpnf}, and the feat is historically ordinary. The
objection's stronger form --- \emph{users will never accept this much ceremony}
--- met a natural experiment in the July 2026 seed-entropy incident
(\S\ref{sec:ethics}): seeds made with $50$-plus fair dice rolls were not at risk
per the vendor's advisory~\cite{coinkite-entropy}, and within days community
guidance converged on that labor-intensive procedure. A second datum sits
in the market for the rival method: holders pay for consulting that builds a
decoy, and rehearse the story until it is believable
(Remark~\ref{rem:decoy-market}). Both data are anecdotal and neither burden is
TKBA's, but together they locate the asymmetry --- TKBA's efficacy is conjectural,
resting on a premise no study has established, whereas the decoy's is condemned
in theory by Kerckhoffs and NNPP and in the record by the registry
(\S\ref{sub:empirical}) --- a record obscurity itself thins, which is why the
condemnation has to rest on the theory (Remark~\ref{rem:anosognosia}). Effort tolerance is not a constant of the population; it
reprices once a threat is legible, and it is already being spent --- on the one of
the two that theory and the record jointly condemn. Making the wrench-attack class
legible \emph{before} the corresponding incident is what a formal bar is for
(Appendix~\ref{app:quantum}).

\textbf{Quantum.}\label{sec:quantum} A quantum search over an $n$-bit space needs
${\sim}2^{n/2}$ \emph{coherent} oracle evaluations~\cite{grover1996}, each here a
deeply sequential Argon2d computation whose full \emph{depth} the coherence must
survive --- a cost the resource-estimation literature finds decisive against far
cheaper oracles~\cite{grassl-grover-aes}. A few-minutes $\Hhard$ already places
Grover out of reach, so sub-256-bit standards are quantum-defensible \emph{without}
a 256-bit seed. The one bound Grover dents --- the cheap $2^{96}$ brute, halved to
${\sim}2^{48}$ --- is confined to the residual window and buys nothing the
classical $2^{32}$ brute there does not (Appendix~\ref{app:quantum}).

\textbf{Stipulations aligned with standard practice.} Two quantities are
stipulated, not derived. The floor rests on the feasibility line
(Stipulation~\ref{ass:feas}) --- a standard \emph{key-size} stipulation; we claim
no reduction to a named hard problem, and none is needed for key sizing. The
coercion-time budget $t$ is read off the registry~\cite{lopp-attacks}, with the
monotonicity that a kidnapping's feasibility \emph{falls} with its duration, so a
\emph{long-hold} adversary sustaining coercion past the gate is out of scope:
against unbounded patience no in-head time-gate suffices. Most recorded attacks
are short hand-over-now events (\S\ref{sub:empirical}).

\textbf{Single-shot utility, and why it is the conservative case.} The kill
decision is modeled once, risk-neutrally --- \emph{worst-for-victim}, since a
risk-averse $\Attacker$ discounts the uncertain gain and kills \emph{less}
readily. \emph{Repeated} encounters are absorbed by the re-key pathway: a victim
who survives with $K$ uncompromised has every incentive to re-key to a fresh
setup. \emph{Inheritance} is beyond scope except for its design target --- the
protocol should render \emph{assassinate-then-coerce-heir} non-viable, simplest
remedy a dead-man switch with heirs also TGPO-protected.

\textbf{Also out of scope.} Adversarial-agent scenarios; device side-channels
during active tacit recall; long-horizon cryptographic erosion; and mass on-chain
correlation beyond standard taproot privacy. Finally, the framework fixes a
\emph{bar}; it does not claim TGPO is the unique construction clearing it, and the
residual window (\S\ref{sec:residual-dr}) is a bounded sliver argued
incentive-safe rather than eliminated --- an incentive argument against
$\mathrm{WDY}[W,c]$, not a theorem, and turning it into a quantitative bound
remains open.
