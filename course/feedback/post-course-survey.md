# Post-Course Survey

> Sent once, after Session 5. Purpose: measure overall outcome attainment,
> capture the make-or-break adoption question ("would you trust real funds to
> this?"), and harvest open critique. ~6–8 minutes. Pair with the pre-course
> survey via handle where possible to measure confidence *gain*.

**Handle (optional):** ______________

---

## A. Outcomes self-assessment *(1–5)*

Rate how true each is now:

1. I can state the **Four Properties** and why conventional self-custody misses
   at least one.
2. I can explain **tacit knowledge** and why it defeats a coercion/wrench attack.
3. I can trace a secret through the **pipeline** (stage-0 → Argon2 → fractal →
   bisection → point) and back.
4. I understand **why the wall holds** (memory-hardness, the chain, the
   parameter choices at a high level).
5. I could perform a **Great Wall setup on my own**, unaided.
6. I can name the system's **assumptions and out-of-scope threats**.

## B. The adoption questions (most important)

7. After doing a setup yourself, how plausible does **"secure your savings with
   memorized fractal locations"** feel now? *(1–5)* — *(compare to pre-course
   Q6)*
8. Would you **trust real funds** to a Great Wall setup today? *(Yes / Yes, after
   more training / Not yet / No)* — Why? *(open)*
9. Would you **recommend this course** to a Bitcoiner friend? *(0–10, NPS-style)*
10. What is your **single biggest remaining doubt** about Great Wall? *(open —
    this is the bug list for the protocol's positioning)*

## C. The course itself

11. Best session, and why? *(open)*
12. Weakest session, and why? *(open)*
13. Was the **textbook** useful alongside the live sessions? *(Didn't read /
    Skimmed / Read & helpful / Read & essential)*
14. Were the **slides** engaging (humor/memes/visuals) or distracting? *(1 = too
    silly … 3 = just right … 5 = too dry)*
15. The **hands-on** parts were: *(too few / right / too many)*; and **reliable to
    run**: *(1–5)*.
16. Length & cadence (5 × 90-min, weekly): *(too short / right / too long)*.

## D. Open critique

17. If you ran this pilot for the next cohort, what is the **first thing you'd
    change**? *(open)*
18. Anything we should **stop / start / continue** doing? *(open)*
19. Free space. *(open)*
