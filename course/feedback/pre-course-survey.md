# Pre-Course Survey

> Sent once, before Session 1. Purpose: calibrate the cohort's background so we
> can **segment** all later feedback (did the material under/over-shoot a given
> group?) and measure confidence *gain* over the course. ~4 minutes.

**Handle (optional).** Discord name, so we can pair this with your later
feedback. Leave blank to stay anonymous.

---

## A. Background

1. How would you rate your familiarity with **Bitcoin self-custody** (seeds,
   hardware wallets, the idea of holding your own keys)? *(1–5)*
2. How would you rate your familiarity with **cryptography concepts** (hashing,
   public-key signatures, key derivation)? *(1–5)*
3. How comfortable are you **using a terminal / building software** (running
   `cargo`, `python`, editing config)? *(1–5)*
4. Have you encountered any of these before? *(check all)*
   - [ ] BIP39 seed phrases
   - [ ] Argon2 / password-hashing / KDFs
   - [ ] Fractals (Mandelbrot/Burning Ship) beyond "pretty pictures"
   - [ ] Time-lock puzzles / VDFs
   - [ ] The "$5 wrench attack" meme
   - [ ] Great Wall specifically (read docs, tried the viewer, etc.)

## B. Stakes & expectations

5. Have you (or someone you know) ever worried about being **physically
   targeted** for crypto holdings? *(Yes / No / Prefer not to say)*
6. Before this course, how plausible does **"secure your savings with memorized
   fractal locations"** sound to you? *(1 = absurd … 5 = totally plausible)*
7. What do you most want to get out of this course? *(open)*
8. What's your biggest **skepticism** about Great Wall going in? *(open)*

## C. Logistics

9. Did you manage to install `great-wall-core` and launch the viewer before
   Session 1? *(Yes / Partially / No / Haven't tried yet)*
10. If not fully: where did it break? *(open — this directly improves the install
    guide)*
11. Anything about the **timing/format** (90-min Discord sessions, weekly) that
    won't work for you? *(open)*
