# Per-Session Feedback Form

> Sent after **every** session. Keep it ~3–5 minutes. Duplicate this form per
> session and fill in the session number and that session's outcomes (from
> [`../syllabus.md`](../syllabus.md)). The confidence pair (Q1/Q2) is the
> backbone metric — track it across all five sessions.

**Session #:** ____  **Title:** ________________  **Handle (optional):** ______

---

## 1. Confidence (the core metric)

For this session's stated outcomes, rate your confidence **before** and
**after** today. *(1–5 each; list the 2–3 outcomes for this session)*

| Outcome | Before | After |
|---------|:------:|:-----:|
| _e.g. "I can explain why obscurity is a cost, not a defense"_ | ☐1–5 | ☐1–5 |
| _outcome 2_ | ☐1–5 | ☐1–5 |
| _outcome 3_ | ☐1–5 | ☐1–5 |

## 2. Muddiest point

What was the **single most confusing thing** today? Be specific — name the
concept, the slide, or the moment. *(open, required)*

## 3. What worked

What's one thing that **helped it click** (an analogy, the demo, a meme, a
pairing)? *(open)*

## 4. Pacing

Today's pace was: *(1 = far too slow · 3 = just right · 5 = far too fast)*
And the live talk-vs-hands-on balance was: *(too much talk / right / too much
hands-on)*

## 5. Hands-on reality check *(if the session had a hands-on block)*

Did the hands-on exercise **actually run on your machine**?
*(Ran fine / Ran with help / Got stuck — couldn't finish / Didn't attempt)*
If it broke, where? *(open — feeds the install/setup fixes)*

## 6. Interaction

The student-to-student interaction (pairs/breakouts) was: *(1 = waste of time …
5 = the best part)*. Why? *(open, optional)*

## 7. ⚑ Pre-registered confusion check

Did **[the flagged ⚑ concept for this session]** confuse you, as we predicted?
*(Yes, badly / A little / No, it was fine)*
> _(Teacher fills the bracket per session — e.g. S3: "the bijection's
> determinism / why no floats"; S4: "why p=1".)_

## 8. Anything else

Open mic — questions, objections, requests, jokes. *(open)*
