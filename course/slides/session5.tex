\gwtitle{Living With It}{Train, accelerate, inherit --- making ``trust your memory'' responsible}

% ── Recap + hook ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}{Three fair fears}
  You now have the whole mechanism. ``Stake your retirement on remembering
  fractals'' still invites three honest objections:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{I'll forget.}
    \item \textbf{The wall is too slow --- for me too.}
    \item \textbf{What happens when I die, or can't recall?}
  \end{enumerate}
  \vspace{4pt}
  \begin{center}\small
  The ecosystem answers each --- \textbf{Train}, \textbf{Accelerate},
  \textbf{Inherit}. Today we also \emph{do a real setup}.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

% ── Train ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}{Train --- don't trust memory you haven't measured}
  \textbf{Celestial Peace: Never Forget} drills your recall with spaced
  repetition (SM-2, Anki-style).
  \begin{keyidea}[Honesty is a feature]
    There's a \textbf{graduation gate}: your stash is \emph{not} considered
    secured until the trainer \emph{measures} that your memory has consolidated.
    The hard question --- ``what if I forget?'' --- is answered by measurement,
    not reassurance.
  \end{keyidea}
  \begin{deeper}[The bootstrap paradox]
    To \emph{secure} a stash you must already recall it; to \emph{memorize} it you
    must train against it. So a \textbf{provisional key} is held externally during
    consolidation and \textbf{destroyed at graduation} --- a transient crutch, not
    a backup.
  \end{deeper}
\end{frame}

% ── Accelerate ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}{Accelerate --- buy back time, keep custody}
  Set your Argon2 delay conservatively ($\sim$2$\times$), then tune the
  \emph{effective} per-session delay with a \textbf{time-lock puzzle (TLP)} ---
  instant to set, chosen wall-clock to solve.
  \begin{itemize}\small
    \item \textbf{Outsource the squaring} to the \textbf{jade-clock} Lightning
      marketplace.
    \item The solver gets only the puzzle --- never your vault --- and learns
      nothing about what it unlocks.
    \item You pay over Lightning (anonymity); \textbf{custody never leaves your
      device}.
  \end{itemize}
  \begin{center}\footnotesize\color{graytext}
  Same posture the training vault uses --- reused on the inheritance path.
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

% ── Inherit ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}{Inherit --- a phoenix, not a lawyer}
  \textbf{phoenix-scroll} is a dead-man's switch:
  \begin{itemize}\small
    \item You periodically \emph{rotate} a private Lightning channel. The
      heir-branch key needs \textbf{two shares}: one the heir derives from
      \emph{their own} Great Wall recall, one you hand off each epoch wrapped in a
      TLP.
    \item While you keep rotating, any heir's in-progress solve is invalidated ---
      \textbf{they can't front-run a living you}.
    \item Rotation \emph{stops} (death / incapacity) $\to$ the latest puzzle
      eventually solves $\to$ the heir claims.
    \item Opaque taproot fallbacks let inheritance \textbf{cascade} without you
      ever knowing your heirs' estate plans.
  \end{itemize}
  \begin{pitfall}[The one genuinely terminal failure]
    No inheritance configured $+$ total loss of recall $=$ unrecoverable, by
    anyone --- the price of true self-custody with no backdoor. Inheritance is the
    \emph{only} post-loss recovery path.
  \end{pitfall}
\end{frame}

% ── The feel ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}{The feel: an instrument, not an app}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \begin{column}{0.58\textwidth}
      Two UX north-stars:
      \begin{itemize}\small
        \item \textbf{Sober, but game-like} --- tactile, quietly rewarding, never
          gimmicky.
        \item \textbf{An instrument, not an app} --- you operate and master it; it
          doesn't think for you.
      \end{itemize}
      \vspace{4pt}
      The deeper bet: \emph{spatial} memory (the \textbf{method of loci}, memory
      palaces) is the most robust trainable human memory --- and the
      fractal-as-territory is literally that. The faculty is \emph{dormant, not
      absent}.
    \end{column}
