# Provisional-key QR templates

Printable **blank** templates for copying a provisional-key QR onto paper by
hand — the air-gapped carrier described in
[`../../next-steps/provisional-key-bootstrapping.md`](../../next-steps/provisional-key-bootstrapping.md).

| File | Key size | QR | Cells | Codewords | Mistakes it survives |
|------|----------|----|-------|-----------|----------------------|
| `128-bit-provisional-key-qr-template.svg` | 128-bit (16 bytes) | v1, level L | 21×21 | 26 | 2 codewords |
| `256-bit-provisional-key-qr-template.svg` | 256-bit (32 bytes) | v2, level L | 25×25 | 44 | 4 codewords |

Each holds what every key's code shares — the finders and their separators,
the timing lines, the dark module and (v2) the alignment pattern, solid —
inside the 4-cell quiet zone. The cells to copy are coloured by codeword, the
format cells grey, over a grid. SVG, 5 mm a cell: it prints sharp at any size.

## Use
1. Print the template for your key size.
2. In the app, save the setup (Main › W) and show the key's QR (Q).
3. Copy each black dot onto the template cell of the same colour and place.
4. Mistakes may touch at most 2 codewords (v1) or 4 (v2): any number of wrong
   cells within one codeword counts once. The grey format cells are protected
   on their own. Work alone, with no cameras.
5. Scan it back to open the setup (Main › E). Destroy the paper at graduation.

## Provenance
Generated by the wallet's `app/tool/qr_templates.dart` (from `app/`:
`dart run tool/qr_templates.dart`), from the same layout the app draws the
key's QR with (`app/lib/src/setup/qr_layout.dart`); the wallet keeps copies
in `templates/`. They carry **no secret**.
